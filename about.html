About ATMOS Weather

Weather information, without the clutter.

☀

A simpler way to check the weather.

ATMOS Weather is a weather web application designed to make everyday weather information easy to find and understand. Instead of filling the screen with unnecessary details, it brings the information you need into one clear view.

You can check current conditions, look ahead at the coming hours and days, save favorite cities, and adjust display preferences.

Data and privacy

Weather information is retrieved from Open-Meteo. Saved cities and preferences are stored in your browser using local storage. Location access is optional and requires your permission.

Weather forecasts can change, so use official local alerts for important safety decisions.
